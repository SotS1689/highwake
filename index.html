Click the sky to take the controls. Nothing can hurt your dragon; rock just bounces you off. Gamepads work too: left stick steers, bumpers roll, triggers speed up and brake, A beats the wings.

About Highwake

Ride a sky-dragon through the high islands. Find the four sky keys, wake the Crown, and see what else is out there.

Privacy. Highwake uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which features get used, so the app can be improved. You don't need an account, and nothing you type into the app is collected. Your data will never be sold or given away.
